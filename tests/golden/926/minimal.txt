Battery Against Specified Victim Not a Peace Officer (Pen. Code, §§ 242, 243(b)–(c)(1))



The defendant is charged with battery against a deputy sheriff.

To prove that the defendant is guilty of this charge, the People must prove that:

1. The defendant willfully touched Jordan Sample in a harmful or offensive manner;

2. When the defendant acted, Jordan Sample was a deputy sheriff and was performing the duties of a deputy sheriff;

AND

3. When the defendant acted, he knew or reasonably should have known, that Jordan Sample was a deputy sheriff who was performing his duties.

Someone commits an act willfully when he or she does it willingly or on purpose. It is not required that he or she intend to break the law, hurt someone else, or gain any advantage.

Making contact with another person, including through his or her clothing, is enough to commit a battery.
