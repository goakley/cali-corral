Evidence of Defendant’s Statements



You have heard evidence that the defendant made an oral and a written statements while the court was not in session. You must decide whether the defendant made any of these statements, in whole or in part. If you decide that the defendant made such a statements, consider the statements, along with all the other evidence, in reaching your verdict. It is up to you to decide how much importance to give to the statements.

Consider with caution any statement made by a defendant tending to show their guilt unless the statement was written or otherwise recorded.
