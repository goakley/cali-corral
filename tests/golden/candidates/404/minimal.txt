Intoxication



If you conclude that the defendant was intoxicated at the time of the alleged crime, you may consider this evidence in deciding whether the defendant:

A. Knew that Jordan Sample intended to commit example target offense;

AND

B. Intended to aid and abet Jordan Sample in committing example target offense.

Someone is intoxicated if he or she took any drug, drink, or other substance that caused an intoxicating effect.
