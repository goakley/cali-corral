Second Degree Murder: Peace Officer (Pen. Code, § 190(b), (c))



If you find the defendant guilty of second degree murder, you must then decide whether the People have proved the additional allegation that he murdered a peace officer.

To prove this allegation the People must prove that:

1. Jordan Sample was a peace officer lawfully performing his duties as a peace officer;

AND

2. When the defendant killed Jordan Sample, the defendant knew, or reasonably should have known, that Jordan Sample was a peace officer who was performing his duties.
