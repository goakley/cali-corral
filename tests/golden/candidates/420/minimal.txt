Withdrawal From Conspiracy



The defendant is not guilty of conspiracy to commit example target offense if he withdrew from the alleged conspiracy before any overt act was committed. To withdraw from a conspiracy, the defendant must truly and affirmatively reject the conspiracy and communicate that rejection, by word or by deed, to the other members of the conspiracy known to the defendant.

The People have the burden of proving beyond a reasonable doubt that the defendant did not withdraw from the conspiracy. If the People have not met this burden, you must find the defendant not guilty of conspiracy.
