Motive



The People are not required to prove that the defendant had a motive to commit the crime charged or example conduct alleged in support. In reaching your verdict you may, however, consider whether the defendant had a motive.

Having a motive may be a factor tending to show that the defendant is guilty or that an allegation or special circumstance is true. Not having a motive may be a factor tending to show the defendant is not guilty or that an allegation or special circumstance is not true.
