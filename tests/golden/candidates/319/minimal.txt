Prior Statements of Unavailable Witness



Jordan Sample did not testify in this trial, but his testimony, taken at another time, was read for you. In addition to this testimony, you have heard evidence that Jordan Sample made another statement.

If you conclude that Jordan Sample made that other statement, you may only consider it in a limited way. You may only use it in deciding whether to believe the testimony of Jordan Sample that was read here at trial. You may not use that other statement as proof that the information contained in it is true, nor may you use it for any other reason.
