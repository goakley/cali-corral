Solicitation of a Minor (Pen. Code, § 653j)



The defendant is charged with soliciting a minor to commit a crime.

To prove that the defendant is guilty of this crime, the People must prove that:

1. The defendant voluntarily asked a minor to commit the crime of example target offense;

2. He intended that the minor commit the crime of example target offense;

AND

3. At the time of the offense, the defendant was at least 18 years old.

To decide whether the defendant intended that the minor commit example target offense, please refer to the separate instructions that I will give you on that crime.

A minor is a person under the age of 18.
