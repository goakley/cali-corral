Solicitation of a Minor (Pen. Code, § 653j)



The defendant is charged in Count 1 with soliciting a minor to commit a crime.

To prove that the defendant is guilty of this crime, the People must prove that:

1. The defendant voluntarily asked or induced a minor to commit the crime of example target offense;

2. She intended that the minor commit the crime of example target offense;

3. At the time of the offense, the defendant was at least 18 years old;

AND

4. The minor received the communication containing the request.

To decide whether the defendant intended that the minor commit example target offense, please refer to the separate instructions that I have given you on that crime.

A minor is a person under the age of 18.

If you find the defendant guilty of solicitation, you must decide how many crimes she solicited. When deciding this question, consider the following factors:

1. Were the crimes solicited part of a plan with a single objective or motive or did each crime solicited have a different objective or motive?

2. Were the crimes solicited to be committed at the same time?

3. Were the crimes solicited to be committed in the same place?

4. Were the crimes solicited to be committed in the same way?

5. Was the payment, if any, for the crimes solicited one amount or were different amounts solicited for each crime?

Consider all of these factors when deciding whether the defendant’s alleged acts were a single crime or example number solicitations alleged by separate crimes of solicitation.
